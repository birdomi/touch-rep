## Protocol

- Seeds [0]; backbone LR fixed at **1e-4**; probe LR 1e-4
- ID: episode-level 4-fold CV over all objects (split seed 42)
- OOD: leave-one-object-out — train on the remaining objects, validate on the held-out one
- Encoders: `scratch`, `d4fe4_prox_ep50` (`local_e80` = ``)
- Metrics: balanced accuracy and macro F1. `±` is across seeds.
